## 4. SEO Service Types

### 4.1 On-Page SEO

On-page SEO involves optimising every element within a web page itself to make it more relevant and readable to search engines.

Key activities:
- SEO title tag optimisation (unique, keyword-rich titles per page)
- Meta description writing (compelling, under 160 characters)
- Header structure (H1, H2, H3 hierarchy)
- Keyword research and natural keyword placement within content
- Image optimisation (descriptive filenames, keyword-rich alt text, compressed file sizes)
- Internal linking structure to pass authority between pages
- URL structure optimisation (clean, descriptive URLs)
- Content quality and relevance improvements
- Page speed optimisation

Himal Tech provides complete on-page SEO for up to 20 pages in a single optimisation pass, with additional pages available at £20 per page.

---

### 4.2 Off-Page SEO

Off-page SEO focuses on building the website's authority and reputation through external signals, primarily backlinks from other websites.

Key activities:
- High-quality backlink acquisition from relevant, authoritative sources
- Guest posting on DA 60+ domains with long history
- Local citation building (consistent Name, Address, Phone listings)
- Citation audit and cleanup (removing or correcting inconsistent listings)
- Directory and listing submission
- Google Business Profile (GBP/GMB) optimisation
- Review management strategy
- Social media signal building
- Link building from natural, editorial sources (white-hat only)

Himal Tech offers guest post services on DA 60 and above domains, with manual, natural traffic methods only. No automated or black-hat link schemes.

---

### 4.3 Technical SEO

Technical SEO addresses the backend architecture of a website to ensure search engines can crawl, index, and rank it effectively.

Key activities:
- Website crawl and error identification
- Broken link detection and repair
- XML sitemap creation and submission to Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster
- Robots.txt configuration
- Canonical tag implementation
- HTTPS/SSL verification and configuration
- Page speed testing and improvement (Core Web Vitals compliance)
- Mobile-friendliness audit and remediation
- Structured data / schema markup
- Redirect management (301s, removal of redirect chains)
- Indexation management (identifying pages incorrectly blocked from indexing)
- JavaScript rendering checks

---

### 4.4 Local SEO

Local SEO improves a business's visibility specifically in geographically relevant search results. This is essential for any business serving customers in a specific area.

Key activities:
- Google Business Profile (GBP) creation, verification, and optimisation
- NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency across all online directories
- Local keyword targeting (e.g. "plumber Woolwich", "accountant Greenwich")
- Local citation building in UK directories
- Google Maps optimisation
- Local link acquisition from relevant regional sources
- Review acquisition strategy
- Local landing page creation and optimisation
- Relevance, Distance, and Prominence signals (Google's local ranking factors)

---

### 4.5 Keyword Research

Keyword research identifies the exact terms potential customers are typing into search engines, enabling the business to create optimised content targeting those queries.

Himal Tech's keyword research process:
1. Identify target audience and business objectives
2. Discover primary, secondary, and long-tail keyword opportunities
3. Analyse competitor keyword rankings
4. Assess keyword difficulty and search volume
5. Map keywords to specific pages on the website
6. Provide a prioritised keyword strategy with a targeting plan

Deliverable: Keyword research reports provided as part of SEO packages or as a standalone service.

---

### 4.6 SEO Audit / Website Audit

The SEO audit is a comprehensive diagnostic of a website's current SEO health and performance.

**Price: £100 (PDF Report)**

The audit includes:
- Deep technical crawl identifying broken links and indexing errors
- Competitor comparison analysis
- Mobile-friendliness assessment
- Site speed testing against 2026 Core Web Vitals standards
- Content quality review
- On-page factor evaluation (titles, meta descriptions, headings, alt text)
- Backlink profile assessment

**Deliverable:** Professional PDF report with a prioritised to-do list, enabling the client to fix SEO issues strategically and track measurable growth over time.

---

### 4.7 Google Business Profile (GBP) Management

This includes:
